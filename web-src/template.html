How to play ↗

The playbook

Listen to the hint. Play × and ÷ first, then + and −, left to right. Choose the matching piano and enter its numeric answer for bonus points. Complete qualifying four-question activity rounds to earn twelve beat steps and verses, grouped into R1–R3. Save each group of four verses to continue. Each writing activity has a literary device and an example. Use Change Literary Device to choose another goal, or See Literary Devices to explore the library. Device progress counts saved work and clears on refresh.
